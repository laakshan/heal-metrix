    PHYSIOLOGICAL HEALING TIMELINE

    4 PHASES
    1 · Hemostasis Minutes to hours. Platelets aggregate and fibrin forms a mesh clot to seal torn vessels.
    2 · Inflammation Days 1–4. Neutrophils & macrophages clear debris. Mild warmth and swelling are normal.
    3 · Proliferation Days 4–21. Granulation tissue and collagen scaffold rebuild; wound margins contract.
    4 · Maturation Weeks to months. Collagen reorganizes, tensile strength improves, scar gradually fades.

    IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY RED FLAGS

    CALL DISPATCH IF:
    • Pulsatile or heavy bleeding that will not stop after 10 minutes of direct, firm pressure
    • Deep, gaping lacerations showing subcutaneous adipose, muscle fascia, tendon, or bone
    • Rapidly spreading angry erythema (red streaks), fever >38°C (100.4°F), or foul odor
    • Penetrating animal/human bites, or puncture from corroded metal/soil
    • Neurovascular deficit: numbness, loss of motor control, or third-degree burns

    METHODOLOGY & TECHNICAL LIMITATIONS

    The HEAL METRIX optical engine samples RGB spectra across the central target region of interest (ROI) and computes chromatic cluster distributions correlating with acute bleeding, dried scab formation, exudate pigmentation, and necrotic tissue. This pipeline does not evaluate subsurface tissue depth, tactile tenderness, or bacterial culture titers. Image sensor optics, ambient lumen variance, and dermatological pigment baselines introduce measurement variance. Use this tool strictly as educational telemetry alongside qualified medical evaluation.

    WHY HEAL METRIX CLINICAL TELEMETRY

    Modern computer-assisted wound care platforms bridge the gap between initial trauma and professional triage. By tracking standardized chromatic metrics over consecutive healing phases, patients and triage nurses can quantify changes that manual visual inspection often overlooks. Immediate location-aware triage coordinates dispatch and maps trauma centers before secondary complications emerge.
